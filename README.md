## 📌 Project Overview
Understanding global emission patterns is critical for setting sustainability goals and framing effective environmental policies[cite: 1]. This project provides actionable insights into regional $\text{CO}_2$ trends, highlighting high-emission hubs vs. lower-emission developing areas[cite: 1].

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Visualizations

### 1. Geographic Distribution (Per Capita Emissions)[cite: 1]
* Visualizes $\text{CO}_2$ emissions per capita across different global regions[cite: 1].
* Highlights high-emission hubs versus lower-emission developing regions[cite: 1].
* Serves as a map for stakeholders to pinpoint key areas requiring policy intervention[cite: 1].

### 2. Regional Emission Trends (2000–2011)[cite: 1]
* **East Asia & Pacific:** Experienced a sharp upward trend, nearly doubling total emissions by 2011 (reaching ~25M kt)[cite: 1].
* **North America & Europe/Central Asia:** Maintained high emission levels but remained relatively plateaued over the decade[cite: 1].
* **Developing Regions (e.g., Sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia):** Displayed steady, lower emission levels[cite: 1].

### 3. Interactive Dashboard[cite: 1]
* Connects geographical map data with historical trend lines[cite: 1].
* Allows dynamic filtering by year and region for deep-dive analysis[cite: 1].

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaways & Next Steps
* **Data-Driven Policies:** Targeted intervention is needed for rapidly expanding industrial hubs[cite: 1].
* **Future Work:** Extend analysis to study the correlation between $\text{CO}_2$ emissions and economic growth metrics like GDP
